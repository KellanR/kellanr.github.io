Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest in the Solar System. It is a gas giant with a mass more than two and a half times that of all the other planets in the Solar System combined. Closer to the Sun, only rocky and metallic materials could survive the heat, forming the small, dense inner planets. Farther out, ices and gases survived, allowing the outer planets to grow into massive gas and ice giants.
