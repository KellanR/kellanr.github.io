Olivia Pope

Olivia Pope is a fictional character in the American television series Scandal. She runs her own firm, though she claims to not be lawyer, but a fixer instead. At Olivia Pope & Associate (OPA), she handles crisis management for high profile clients by either making their problem go away, or bring attention to the media for the greater good of her clients. Formerly, she was the White House Communications Director. She is known for her intelligence, resourcefulness, and ability to handle difficult situations.
