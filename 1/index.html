Olivia Pope

Olivia Pope is a fictional character in the American television series Scandal, portrayed by Kerry Washington. She is a former White House Communications Director who starts her own crisis management firm, Olivia Pope & Associates. The character is known for her intelligence, resourcefulness, and ability to handle high-profile scandals and political crises.

  • Full Name: Olivia Carolyn Pope
  • Occupation: Crisis Manager, Former White House Communications Director
  • Portrayed by: Kerry Washington
  • First Appearance: Scandal (2012)
