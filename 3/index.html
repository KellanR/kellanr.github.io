Solar System Explorer

Topic Description

The solar system, to us, is an infinite void that contains plentiful celestial object. To be more specific, the solar system is a collection of planets, dwarf planets, moons, and smaller bodies that orbit our sun. These are the common foundational knowledge about our solar system that was taught in our school, but nobody knows how many moons a specific planet other than earth has. The typical student just has to remember the basics about the very object they live on and its neighbors for a test, then it is just flushed out. Sources have web pages to share this information, but sometimes the terminology or the navigation of the website can be more for the average user, which can deter them from trying to learn about the topic.

This proposal is a way to bridge the gap in the knowledge of today's society on our solar system and to reinforce more information about it. This project will inform you of the deeper facts on the objects in space, such as, their size, diameter, and moons, etc. There are plentiful credible resources out there about this topic especially from NASA and the American Astronomical Society to inform us about new discoveries and the history of space exploration. This application is not to challenge the credibility of the sources, but to give interactive experience with the information to casual learners instead of the standard textbook format that can be too dull for the eyes and for the mind to focus.

Application Description

Home Page

This will house the landing page of Solar System Explorer. It would contain a welcome message, a navigation to get to all of the pages that go along with the Solar System Explorer.

Two-Step Interactive

This core page will guide users through ah interactive two-step process. First, choices between inner and outer planets. The next choices would be the range of planets within the click selection. Then, drop down and reveal details about each one. After all the choices were made, the image of the item selected would show up and attributes(diameter, day cycle, number of moons, etc).

Read-only Data

This information will provide details about the hypothosis about how our solar system was created.

Contact Us

The fourth page will be the Contact Us page. The information I could collect is name, email address, questions or reprts about the data, or recommendations on feature additions.
